# Phase 1 — Research Foundation & Instrument Development

## Project

**The Knowledge–Practice Gap in Islamic Ritual Observance**  
*A Cross-Sectional Study of Wudu Knowledge, Visible Practice, and Mosque-Level Teaching Exposure in Riyadh*

**Current Stage:** Phase 1 (Research & Instrument Development)

**Target Duration:** 1–3 months

**Estimated Budget:** 0–500 SAR

---

# Mission

The objective of Phase 1 is **not** to collect data.

The objective is to build a complete academic foundation that is ready for:

- Scholarly review
- Ethical approval
- Pilot testing
- Future funding

At the end of Phase 1, the study should be ready for a small pilot without requiring major redesign.
